The logo design has the following contributions and terms:

| Component | Attribution | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inherited Anki star | Alex Fraser | [AGPL-3.0-or-later](ANKI_STAR_LICENSE.md) |
| Original AnkiDroid logo design | Tom Svoboda | [Public-domain declaration](../../LICENSES/LicenseRef-AnkiDroid-Logo-Public-Domain.txt) (`LicenseRef-AnkiDroid-Logo-Public-Domain`) |
| Later AnkiDroid adaptations | Contributors recorded in Git history | [GPL-3.0-or-later](../../LICENSES/GPL-3.0-or-later.txt) |

Tom's declaration covers his original contribution and does not specify CC0.
The [inherited Anki star](ANKI_STAR_LICENSE.md) has its own copyright and
licence.

The same entries are used for both pictured versions, based on their shared
lineage and AnkiDroid's licensing. Martin Disch describes the
launcher version as an adaptation of the previous SVG in [his proposal](https://github.com/ankidroid/Anki-Android/issues/5110).
The [2019 SVGs](https://github.com/ankidroid/Anki-Android/blob/625be8dd59863d42028a4ca61a9b6cfdcf98112f/docs/graphics/src/anki.svg)
contain no separate licence notice: the GPL entry for later contributions is
inferred from AnkiDroid's [contemporaneous GPL-3.0-or-later notice](https://github.com/ankidroid/Anki-Android/blob/f19ef85446b93fe8ff19e45fb01ff2e27dbd89ae/AnkiDroid/src/main/java/com/ichi2/anki/AnkiDroidApp.java#L6-L10),
rather than an artwork-specific declaration from each contributor.
Tom's statement does not place other contributors' modifications in the public domain.
